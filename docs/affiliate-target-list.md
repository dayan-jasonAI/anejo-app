## Fitness creators — read this

I did **not** produce Instagram handles for local creators, and I want to be direct about why: the
sources that rank "Palm Beach fitness influencers" are paid aggregator databases that didn't expose
verified handles in search. Anything I wrote down would be a plausible-looking guess — and you'd be
DMing strangers, or worse, the wrong person, with the Añejo name attached.

**Find them yourself in about 20 minutes, with better results:**
1. Instagram → search **places**: "Wellington, Florida", "Palm Beach Gardens", "Jupiter, Florida",
   "Delray Beach" → filter to fitness posts, note who posts consistently.
2. Hashtags: `#wellingtonfl` `#palmbeachfitness` `#jupiterfl` `#561fitness` `#bocafitness`
3. **Check the tagged photos of every Tier-1 gym above** — their members who post are already local,
   already fitness, already bought in. This is the single highest-yield source.
4. Target **2k–50k followers**. A 4k-follower Wellington trainer converts better than a 200k national
   account, because their audience can actually order from you.
5. Verify before contact: do they post from PBC in the last 30 days? Real engagement (comments, not
   just likes)? Would you eat what they already recommend?

## Before you send

- **Confirm prices against the HUB** (`/hub/owner/menu.html`) the morning you send. See the price
  verification note — the HUB is the source of truth, and a wrong price in an outreach email becomes
  a promise you have to honor.
- **Verify the business still exists.** CrossFit Palm Beach (Jupiter) shows as **CLOSED** in current
  listings — proof these directories go stale. One dead link makes you look careless.
- **Find the owner's actual name.** "Hey [gym]" reads like a blast. "Hey Christine" reads like a
  person. Instagram bio, About page, or the Google listing.
- **DM first, email second.** DM gets the yes/no; email carries the numbers.
- **Log who you contacted and when.** Two follow-ups maximum, then stop.
