## The lead: renewal economics

Most affiliate offers pay once. Ours pays **every week the person stays subscribed** — so a partner
builds an income floor, not a spike. That is the whole pitch, and it should be the first thing in
every message.

**10% commission, including every renewal:**

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Añejo Weekly · 5 bowls | $99 | $9.90 | $42.90 | **$514.80** |

Ten members on the 10-bowl plan ≈ **$9,828/year recurring**. Twenty-five ≈ **$24,570/year**.

**Always say "as long as they stay subscribed."** Never present these as guaranteed income — they
are the rate, not a promise. Overstating it is how partnerships end badly.

**Payout:** monthly cash, or take it as Añejo credit at **1.5×** ($100 → $150).
**Their audience gets:** free Añejo Fit drink on the first order + **2× rewards points for life**.
No discount code to devalue the brand — an upgrade instead of a markdown.

# RULES

**FTC disclosure is not optional.** Every partner must disclose. `#ad` or `#AñejoPartner` visible
without tapping "more" — not buried in hashtags, not in a story only. Put it in the welcome pack and
say it out loud on the call. Their exposure and ours.

**Never send bulk from the Añejo domain.** These go one-to-one from your personal inbox or DM.
Blasting cold contacts from the domain that carries receipts and magic links puts real customer mail
in spam. The campaign tool in the HUB is for people who opted in — not for cold outreach.

**No health claims.** Not from you, not from partners. Freshness, macro accuracy, convenience, taste.
Nothing about weight, disease, or treatment.

**W-9 before the first payout.** No exceptions. Commission is taxable income and the paperwork has to
exist before money moves, not after.

**Don't promise exclusivity or territory** in a cold message. If someone asks, that's a real
conversation with real terms — not a line in an email.

# SEQUENCE

1. **Build the list — 30 names.** Palm Beach County only: gyms and boxes (Wellington, Palm Beach
   Gardens, Jupiter, West Palm, Boca, Delray), independent trainers, creators 2k–50k followers. Small
   accounts convert better than big ones; a 4k-follower local trainer beats a 200k national account
   for this.
2. **Warm before you ask.** Follow, watch stories, leave two real comments over a few days. Cold DMs
   from an unknown account get ignored or land in requests.
3. **DM first, email second.** DM for the yes-or-no, email for the numbers.
4. **Feed them before they post.** Send a real week of food to anyone serious. A partner who has
   eaten it recommends it differently, and you'll know fast whether they'd actually recommend it.
5. **Onboard the yes** at `/hub/owner/partners.html` → **Create partner** — generates their code and
   sends the welcome pack in one click.
6. **Check `rev_share_events` monthly**, pay from the same page (**Mark paid**), and tell them what
   they earned. Partners who see the money coming keep posting.

**Realistic expectation:** 30 contacts → ~10 replies → ~3–5 partners. That is a good outcome, not a
bad one.

Three partners who each keep five members on the 10-bowl plan is 15 subscribers — about
**$147,000/yr in revenue to Añejo**, against **$14,742/yr paid out** in commission. That 10% is the
cheapest customer acquisition in the business: it costs nothing until it works, and it costs nothing
if it doesn't.
