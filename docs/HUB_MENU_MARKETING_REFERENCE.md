## One family, three categories

Añejo Catering: trays, celebrations, team meals and custom events. Añejo Traditional: Cuban comfort meals, small bites, sandwiches, sides and desserts. Añejo Fit: seven Mediterranean-Cuban bowls and three drinks. La Cajita is the newest family addition. MAR salmon is the Fit cover. Traditional uses plated lechón and authentic purple-brown, distinct-grain congrí. Keep the top navigation Cajita link and prominent photography for every category.

## Exact portions and product distinctions

Owner-confirmed weights: cold salad 6 oz; croqueta 1.20 oz fried; empanada 1.25 oz; lechón 6 oz; tamal three slices about 5.5 oz; congrí 8 oz by weight. Fresh salad does not inherit the cold salad weight. Other launch portions and costing remain estimates pending actual recipe yields, labor, packaging and waste measurement.

Use one customizable family per flavor-based item. Separate regular/dressed croqueta prices. No standalone salami bite. Skewer sequence: grape, ham or salami, cheese, guava, pineapple. Keep the same full skewer in Cajita and tray formats. Lunch/dinner, appetizers, sides, desserts, catering, Fit and sauces are separate shopping sections.
