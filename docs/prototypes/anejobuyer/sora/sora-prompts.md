Same customer, same clothing, same desk, same late-morning light, same Añejo FUEGO steak bowl, same cream/black/forest-green/gold brand palette, same clean delivery bag, same packaging, same bowl quantity across cuts.

## Shot Prompts

### Shot 1 - Pressure

Create a 1.4-second silent cinematic shot of a believable health-conscious professional, age 28-45, seated at a realistic South Florida office desk during late morning. Subject wears a forest-green or charcoal button-up shirt, neutral trousers, simple watch, natural grooming, not a luxury model. They answer a phone call while scanning a laptop and a busy message screen, looking focused but slightly pressed for time. Camera: medium close desk angle, 35mm documentary look, slow lateral slide from left to right. Lighting: soft late-morning window light with warm practical fill. Environment: modern small business, real-estate, or co-working office, not a corporate stock-photo tower. Emotional tone: composed pressure, subtle hunger. Aspect ratio 16:9, center-safe for mobile crop. Exclude visible brand logos except Añejo later, no melodrama, no luxury signals, no gym imagery.

### Shot 2 - Hunger Distraction

Create a 1.3-second silent close-up of the same customer's hand near a phone, notebook, and laptop calendar at 12:18 PM. The person glances at the time and returns to work, showing hunger as a small distraction. Camera: 50mm shallow depth close-up, slight push-in. Same wardrobe, same desk, same late-morning lighting. Keep phone UI generic and unreadable except time-like context if needed. No fake app brand, no exaggerated frustration, no empty fast-food wrapper, no medical or diet imagery. Physical realism: natural fingers, plausible hand position, no warped phone.

### Shot 3 - Careful Preparation

Create a 1.8-second silent food-prep shot on a clean warm kitchen prep surface. A real Añejo FUEGO-style bowl is assembled with precision: sliced chimichurri steak placed as hero protein, quinoa or brown rice base, roasted zucchini, roasted peppers, arugula/greens, pickled red onions, pepitas, microgreens, sauce cup on side. Camera: overhead-to-angled 50-70mm food close-up, controlled top-down glide. Lighting: warm kitchen task light, food colors honest and vibrant. Tone: premium, fresh, cooked today, generous but clean. Exclude floating ingredients, impossible motion, sterile white lab kitchen, synthetic food shine, frozen trays, bulk meal-prep stacks.

### Shot 4 - Personalization

Create a 1.2-second silent macro close-up at the packout counter. The same bowl is finished with sauce cup and a small understated prep note, such as "brown rice swap" or "macro portion," implying the meal was prepared for the customer without relying only on a name. Camera: 70mm detail shot with rack focus from note to sauce cup to bowl label. Lighting: warm controlled kitchen light. Packaging: matte bowl, clean lid, cream/forest/black/gold Añejo color cues. If logo accuracy is uncertain, keep logo area simple and leave room for edit overlay. Exclude illegible AI text, misspelled Añejo logo, medical claims, calorie promises, or intrusive personalization.

### Shot 5 - Direct Delivery

Create a 1.2-second silent shot of the sealed Añejo bowl being placed into a simple dark or forest-green local delivery bag and leaving the prep counter. Camera: 35mm close follow shot, smooth handheld-stable movement. Lighting transitions from warm kitchen to daylight. Food remains upright and protected. The delivery feels local and same-day. Exclude shipping boxes, dry ice, ice packs, cardboard meal kits, weekly bulk delivery, generic app branding, or chaotic motion.

### Shot 6 - Arrival

Create a 1.2-second silent medium handoff shot at the same customer's office door or reception desk. The customer receives the clean delivery bag without leaving the workday or having a long interaction. Same customer, same wardrobe, same office, late-morning/lunch lighting. Camera: 35mm static frame with slight push-in. Tone: smooth, reliable, local, low disruption. Exclude exaggerated smiling handshake, staged luxury lobby, driver theatrics, wrong customer, or changed clothing.

### Shot 7 - Reveal and First Bite

Create a 1.5-second silent close-up-to-reaction shot. The same customer opens the bowl at their desk or small break table, revealing the same FUEGO bowl with steak, grains, roasted vegetables, greens, pickled onions, pepitas, microgreens, and sauce cup. Steam only if physically plausible. Customer adds a small amount of sauce and takes one bite. Camera: 50mm gentle push-in, then a natural reaction. Emotional tone: relief, satisfaction, focus returning. Exclude impossible steam, fake plastic-looking food, warped utensils, extra fingers, messy overflow, or magical transformation.

### Shot 8 - Brand Close

Create a 0.9-second closing frame for an Añejo homepage hero. Show the customer's desk with the fresh bowl in foreground, customer refocused in soft background, warm natural office light, Añejo color palette. Leave clean negative space for an editor to overlay the real Añejo logo and the approved line: "Clean Fuel. Bold Flavors. Cooked Fresh. Delivered Daily." Camera static, no distracting movement. Aspect ratio 16:9, center-safe for mobile crop. If the model generates text, it must be minimal and spelled correctly; safer option is no generated text, leaving all logo and copy for edit overlay.

## Alternative Prompts for Risky Shots

### Alternative for Shot 3 if Food Looks Synthetic

Create a real-food documentary close-up of an already assembled Añejo FUEGO-style bowl on a warm prep counter. Do not show ingredient placement in motion. Focus on real textures: seared steak grain, quinoa, roasted vegetables, greens, pickled red onions, pepitas, microgreens, sauce cup. Use gentle camera push-in and natural kitchen light. Avoid any animated ingredient movement.

### Alternative for Shot 4 if Text or Logo Fails

Create a close-up of a blank clean prep label with only simple colored lines and checkmarks, no readable words, beside sauce cup and sealed bowl. Leave space for editor-added text overlay. Maintain Añejo cream, black, forest green, and muted gold color treatment. No generated logo.

### Alternative for Shot 6 if Character Continuity Fails

Shoot handoff without showing faces: same customer's torso and hands receive the delivery bag at a desk or doorway. Match wardrobe from reference frame: forest-green or charcoal shirt, simple watch. Keep the environment consistent with the office reference.

### Alternative for Shot 8 if Logo Accuracy Fails

Generate no logo and no text. Create only a clean closing visual with food foreground, customer refocused in background, dark/forest negative-space area on left or right for edit overlay. The real Añejo logo and CTA must be composited during editing.
