# Añejo Homepage Hero Video Creative Brief

Date: 2026-08-04  
Use: Sora-ready production package for `/prototype/anejobuyer/` homepage hero  
Status: Ready for generation when an authorized video workflow exists

## Video Purpose

Create a silent autoplay homepage hero loop that makes a first-time visitor immediately understand that Añejo cooks flavorful real food fresh today and delivers it directly into a busy day. The video should reinforce the page headline and CTA, not compete with them.

## Target Viewer

A busy, health-conscious professional in Palm Beach County, approximately 28-45 years old. They have disposable income but evaluate value carefully. They want fresh, flavorful, high-quality food and do not want nutrition, grocery shopping, or cooking to become another daily project.

## Desired Viewer Reaction

- "That looks like my day."
- "I do not have time to leave and find food."
- "That food looks fresh, warm, healthy, and flavorful."
- "Añejo would make this part of my day easier."
- "This is not frozen meal prep."
- "This feels personal and dependable."

## Brand Positioning

Use the existing positioning exactly:

Clean Fuel. Bold Flavors. Cooked Fresh. Delivered Daily.

Do not invent a new slogan or replacement brand identity.

## Narrative

The story follows one professional through a busy late morning. They are focused and pressed for time, the Añejo bowl is prepared carefully, a subtle personalization cue appears, the meal travels directly to their location, and the customer receives, opens, eats, relaxes slightly, and returns to work with renewed focus.

## Visual Tone

Cinematic but natural. Premium but not artificial. Warm but not nostalgic. Modern but not sterile. Energetic without frantic editing. Cuban-American influence should come through flavor, confidence, warmth, hospitality, and rhythm rather than costumes, flags, props, or caricature.

## Customer Description

One believable professional, age 28-45. Polished everyday workwear. No luxury-fashion styling, no fitness influencer styling, no exaggerated distress. The person is hungry and mentally loaded before the meal, then subtly relieved and refocused afterward.

## Location Description

Primary location: realistic professional environment such as a real estate office, small business workspace, co-working office, or private office with a meeting table and laptop. It should feel like South Florida work life without looking like a stock-photo corporate tower.

Secondary location: clean prep/kitchen surface and local delivery handoff. Do not show an exact kitchen street address or regulated facility details.

## Food Description

Use one consistent Añejo bowl for continuity. Recommended bowl: FUEGO Steak Bowl, because the existing prototype hero uses FUEGO and the image communicates premium protein, roasted vegetables, greens, pickled onion, quinoa, chimichurri, pepitas, and microgreens.

Food must look real, abundant, fresh, sectioned, and edible. Sauce should be controlled and/or on the side. Steam may appear only during opening if physically plausible.

## Wardrobe

Customer: clean modern workwear, neutral shirt or blouse, understated watch, no loud luxury labels, no gymwear. Driver/handoff: simple dark or forest-green delivery layer or clean polo, no invented uniform claims if logo accuracy cannot be controlled.

## Props

Laptop, phone, notebook, calendar page or appointment list, clean desk, reusable water bottle, Añejo bowl, sauce cup, napkin, utensil, delivery bag, small prep label with a preference note.

## Lighting

Late morning to lunch light. Office: soft natural daylight with warm practical fill. Kitchen: clean warm task light, not fluorescent laboratory white. Food reveal: warm enough to feel fresh, with accurate color and texture.

## Color Treatment

Match Añejo palette: near black, forest green, cream, muted brass/gold. Keep food colors vibrant and honest. Avoid orange-and-teal blockbuster grading, heavy blue corporate tint, or oversaturated artificial food color.

## Pacing

Hero loop: 8-12 seconds, silent, complete basic story, fast but legible.  
Expanded version: 20-30 seconds, more breathing room and clearer personalization.

## Prohibited Imagery

No frozen food, shipping boxes, dry ice, ice packs, weekly bulk meal-prep stacks, meal kits, microwaves, medical nutrition imagery, body-transformation imagery, measuring tape, scale cliches, gym montages, fake reviews, fake testimonials, generic luxury kitchen, white lab-style kitchen, floating ingredients, impossible food movement, or caricatures of Cuban culture.

## Continuity Requirements

- Same customer, clothing, hair, office, meal, packaging, delivery bag, lighting progression, and time of day.
- Same bowl ingredients and quantity across prep, delivery, and eating.
- Añejo logo/wordmark must be accurate if shown. If logo rendering fails, use a clean non-logo label and reserve the actual approved logo for edit overlay.
- Hand and utensil movements must be physically plausible.
- Customer's emotional change must be subtle: hunger/friction to relief/focus, not melodrama.
