# Sora Reference Frame Plan

Purpose: define still frames and approved references before generating video, so character, food, packaging, color, and layout remain consistent.

## Character Sheet

- Create or source one reference image of the primary customer: age 28-45, polished but realistic, health-conscious professional, neutral/forest wardrobe, natural expression.
- Required views: front three-quarter, desk-working pose, receiving-bag pose, first-bite pose.
- Avoid: luxury-model styling, fitness influencer posture, exaggerated stress.

## Wardrobe Reference

- Forest-green or charcoal button-up shirt, neutral trousers, simple watch, clean everyday workwear.
- Use the same wardrobe in all customer shots.
- No luxury labels, gymwear, or overly formal suit styling.

## Office Reference

- Realistic South Florida professional workspace: laptop, phone, notebook, water bottle, calendar context.
- Lighting: late morning daylight, warm fill.
- Must be believable for office professional, entrepreneur, or real-estate professional.
- Avoid: glass corporate stock-photo boardroom, mansion office, generic luxury set.

## Kitchen Reference

- Clean warm prep counter, not a sterile lab kitchen.
- Surfaces should be cream, matte black, forest green, warm neutral, or stainless used sparingly.
- Do not show exact kitchen address or regulated facility details.

## Food Hero Reference

Reuse approved repository food photography whenever possible:

- Primary: `/public/assets/img/bowl_fuego.jpg`
- Secondary texture/color references: `/public/assets/img/bowl_mar.jpg`, `/public/assets/img/bowl_vida.jpg`, `/public/assets/img/bowl_coco.jpg`
- Sauce references: `/public/assets/img/sauce_chimichurri_vital.jpg`, `/public/assets/img/sauce_ajo_citrico.jpg`

The real Añejo food photos should guide generated motion. Do not replace suitable food photography with invented food design.

## Packaging Reference

- Matte bowl/lid, clean sauce cup on side, compact prep label.
- Use cream/black/forest/gold color cues.
- If Sora cannot render the logo accurately, leave branding plain and add the approved logo in editing.

## Delivery Bag Reference

- Simple insulated local delivery bag in dark/forest color.
- It should feel clean, direct, and same-day.
- Avoid shipping boxes, ice packs, dry ice, weekly bulk bags, or generic app branding.

## Color and Lighting Reference

- Añejo palette: near black `#0D0D0D`, forest green `#1A3D2E`, cream `#F5F2EC`, muted gold `#C8BC6E`.
- Food should retain bright, honest ingredient color.
- Grade should be warm and premium without orange-and-teal exaggeration.

## Opening Frame

- Customer in office, phone/laptop active, late morning pressure.
- Leave headline-safe negative space if used under homepage text.
- Must still communicate "busy day" without sound.

## Closing Frame

- Bowl visible in foreground, customer refocused in background, clean negative space for approved Añejo logo and positioning overlay.
- Preferred final overlay should be added in edit, not generated by the model.
