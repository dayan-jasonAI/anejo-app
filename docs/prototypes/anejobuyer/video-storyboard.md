# Añejo Buyer Hero Video Storyboard

Purpose: prototype a muted looping hero treatment without generating production video or inventing unsupported production assets.

## Shot list

| Shot | Duration | Framing | Subject | Environment | Food details | Motion | On-screen copy | Transition |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2.0s | Medium, desk-level | Busy professional between calls | Believable office or real-estate/co-working setting | Empty desk space, water, laptop | Customer checks time and messages | "Busy day. Lunch still matters." | Hard cut on phone glance |
| 2 | 2.5s | Overhead close-up | Kitchen assembly | Clean prep table | FUEGO steak, quinoa, roasted vegetables, chimichurri | Hands place ingredients in clean sections | "Cooked fresh today." | Match cut from desk to bowl |
| 3 | 2.0s | Macro close-up | Packaging | Kitchen packout counter | Sauce cup on side, label, sealed bowl | Lid closes, label applied | "Packed clean. Sauce on the side." | Quick push-in |
| 4 | 1.5s | Tracking shot | Delivery handoff | Car/loadout or delivery bag | Branded bag or clean insulated carrier | Bag moves from kitchen to route | "Delivered daily." | Motion blur wipe |
| 5 | 2.0s | Medium | Customer receives order | Office reception, lobby, desk, gym, or property office | Bag handed to customer | Customer receives with natural expression | "Where your day actually happens." | Cut on handoff |
| 6 | 2.5s | Close and reaction | Customer opens meal | Desk or break area | Warm/fresh bowl, visible protein, sauce | Lid opens, steam/freshness cue if real | "Clean fuel. Bold flavor." | Slow dissolve |
| 7 | 1.5s | Medium | Customer resumes work focused | Same professional environment | Bowl beside laptop | Customer takes first bite then returns to work | "Order today." | Loop to shot 1 |

## Sound-design recommendation

Muted by default for web. If production later includes sound, use subtle kitchen/packaging texture, light route ambience, and no intrusive music. Never autoplay audio.

## AI-generation notes

- Use real-looking South Florida professionals, not luxury archetypes or fitness stereotypes.
- Keep offices and delivery scenes believable.
- Food must look like actual Añejo bowls: bright ingredients, premium protein, clean sectional plating, sauce controlled/on side.
- Wardrobe should be modern and normal, not staged luxury.
- Include a range of secondary customers across office, fitness, entrepreneurship, and real-estate-adjacent environments.

## Continuity requirements

- Same order must plausibly move from kitchen to customer.
- Same bowl style, label language, and packaging should appear across shots.
- Time of day should support lunch or dinner delivery window.
- Customer mood should shift from rushed/hungry to relieved/focused.

## What must never appear

- Frozen trays, microwave-only meal-prep stacks, generic stock meal prep boxes, medical diet imagery, weight-loss claims, fake reviews, celebrity/luxury staging, unrealistic wealth signals, extreme fitness stereotypes, public kitchen address, or unapproved production facility details.
