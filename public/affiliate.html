The Founding Creators Program

Our affiliate program for creators, foodies, athletes and local voices in South Florida. You get a personal code and link; every order it touches earns you commission — including weekly meal-plan renewals, month after month, for as long as your people stay subscribed. Most affiliate programs pay you once; ours pays you every week they stay.

How it works

  1. Apply. One email with your name, handle and a line about your audience.
  2. Get approved. We review every partner personally — this is a small, real program, not a mass link farm.
  3. Receive your code + link. Yours alone, live immediately, trackable from day one.
  4. Earn monthly. Commission starts at 10% of every sale on your code — paid monthly in cash, or take it as Añejo credit at 1.5× value ($100 becomes $150 of Añejo).

What your audience gets

  • A free Añejo Fit cold-press drink on their first order with your code
  • Double rewards points on that first order

Minimum requirements

  • A real, engaged audience in food, fitness, wellness or South Florida life — we care about engagement and fit, not a follower count
  • Your audience can actually order: Palm Beach County reach, or nearby South Florida
  • 18 or older
  • Content that fits the brand: premium, honest, no medical or weight-loss claims
  • Clear disclosure of the partnership in your posts (FTC rules — we'll show you the simple way)

Gyms & Trainers Program

You coach the training. We handle the food — built to each client's macro targets, not a one-size menu. Your clients get goal-sized meal plans in 5, 10 or 12-bowl weeks, portioned from their numbers, delivered across Palm Beach County.

How the partnership works

  • You're part of the plan: trainers can be attached to their clients' Añejo plans — with accountability check-ins that help clients actually follow through on eating the way they train
  • Clients start with the free macro calculator, subscribe directly, and manage everything themselves — pause, skip a week, cancel anytime
  • Gym and studio partners earn on the clients they bring — same rails as our creator program, built for recurring weekly plans
  • Partner perks for your space: tastings, gym-exclusive bowls and corporate wellness menus are part of how we work with the right partners
